# Automotive Art Direction Skill
## Primary Rule
The car content is always the hero. Never let the site feel like a generic portfolio.
## Visual
- Deep black/graphite backgrounds, warm off-white secondary sections.
- Signal orange accent (CSS var --accent, configurable).
- Full-bleed 16:9/21:9 cinematic compositions.
- Thin technical borders, subtle grain, generous whitespace.
- No excessive gradients, neon, glassmorphism or rounded UI.
## Typography
- Display: heavy condensed cinematic sans (Bebas Neue / Monument Extended).
- Body: clean modern sans (Inter, Outfit).
- Metadata: monospace/technical grotesk.
- Fluid sizing with clamp().
## Site Must Feel Like
Premium automotive production studio, luxury car campaign, film title sequence.
## Never Feel Like
Generic SaaS, developer portfolio, gaming dashboard, cyberpunk UI.
